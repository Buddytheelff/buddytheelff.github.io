About Me

Network Engineer with 7+ years of progressive experience supporting and improving multi-state research infrastructure at NCAR/UCAR. I began my career as an Intern and advanced through Network Technician to eventually Network Engineer. I have hands on experience spanning network operations, infrastructure implementation, troubleshooting and engineering.

Currently pursuing a Master of Science in Network Engineering at the University of Colorado Boulder, with a focus on enterprise networking, network automation, cloud infrastructure, and technology management. My interests increasingly center on how technology, automation, and effective teams can work together to build reliable and scalable infrastructure.

My background in Geography and Geographic Information Systems (GIS) also gives me a strong perspective on spatial data, infrastructure documentation, and complex systems. I enjoy solving challenging technical problems, improving processes, and helping develop the people and systems around me.
